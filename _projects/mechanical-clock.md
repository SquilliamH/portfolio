We were given the escapement wheel and pallet dimensions and asked to design our own pendulum, then predict the clock's timing and compare it with the real thing. I designed mine as a person riding a Onewheel, kept symmetric, with the nuts and bolts gathered in a circle at the bottom so it would behave as close to a point mass as possible.

One revolution of the escapement wheel measured 9.94 s. Point-mass analysis predicted 8.43 s (15% error) and rigid-body analysis predicted 6.52 s (34% error). The rigid-body model is the more realistic one, so it should have been closer; the report works through why it wasn't.
