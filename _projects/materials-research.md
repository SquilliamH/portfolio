Our group researched materials for an accessory dwelling unit that Triton Solar Decathlon planned to build in San Diego's Logan Heights for a low-income community. We compared options for the roof, floor, and interior and exterior walls on affordability, desirability, producibility, ecological sustainability, usability, and architectural fit, using a cost spreadsheet and a weighted decision matrix.
