Current Mars rovers are large and mostly limited to the surface, and Ingenuity is small but can only fly in open space. Our proposal, GeckoRov, asked whether a miniature robot could combine a wall-climbing robot with a self-folding soft robot, to fit through small spaces and climb the walls of caves and holes.

I was principal investigator for the ten-person team. The research plan looked at integrating shape-memory polymers with micro-grippers and at how the result would compare to existing Mars exploration robots, with a target of less than 10 lb and less than one-fifth the volume of a vehicle like Ingenuity.
