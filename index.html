About

I design and build robotic systems for emergency response and medical robotics. My research centers on legged locomotion, physical human-robot interaction, and mechanisms that adapt their behavior to the task. Most of my time goes into the hardware itself: CAD, machining, motor bring-up over CAN, and the testing that shows whether a design actually works.

Before grad school I led mechanical design for Triton Robotics and later served as its president. I finish my MS in 2027.

Design and fabrication

SolidWorks, Creo, Ansys FEA, Fusion 360 CAM, DFMA, CNC and manual machining, laser and waterjet cutting, rapid prototyping

Electronics and controls

PCB design (EasyEDA), BLDC motor control, CAN, circuit design

Software

Python, MATLAB, C (Arduino), ROS 2, embedded Linux
