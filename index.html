What I'm working on

A leg that changes shape

A search and rescue robot has to cross rough ground quickly, then pull a heavy load once it reaches someone. This leg does both by changing its link lengths on the fly. The paper is accepted at ICRA 2026.

How I work

Build it to find out

I take ideas from CAD to working hardware: aluminum frames, capstan drives, TPU-printed grippers, and the motor control that runs them over CAN.

Check the model against reality

A lift mechanism's predicted stall load landed within about 1% of the measured one, and a chipboard desk failed within 6% of its FEA prediction. I compare, then explain the gap.

Design for contact with people

A lot of my work touches a person: a soft gripper for human limbs, a vacuum-stiffening limb splint, and a haptic teleoperation device.
